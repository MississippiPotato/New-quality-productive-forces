    概念与政策脉络

    Concept and policy timeline

    AI 如何改变生产力的要素？

    How does AI change the factors of production?

    先建立劳动资料、劳动对象、劳动者与优化组合的阅读框架，再用各章数据查看变化。

    Start with tools, tasks, workers and their combination, then examine the changes through data.

    新质生产力“以劳动者、劳动资料、劳动对象及其优化组合的跃升为基本内涵，以全要素生产率大幅提升为核心标志”。 —— 2024 年 1 月 31 日，中央政治局第十一次集体学习
    New quality productive forces “take as their basic substance the leap in workers, means of labour, objects of labour and their optimal combination, and a substantial rise in total factor productivity as their core hallmark.” — 11th group study session of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee, January 2024 (English rendering by this site)

    一条政策时间线

    国家算力枢纽节点、国家数据中心集群获批，“东数西算”在 2022 年 2 月全面启动。基础设施先行，为后来的智能算力爆发埋下伏笔。

    A policy timeline

    With national computing hubs and national data-centre clusters approved, East Data, West Computing was launched in full in February 2022. Infrastructure came first, laying the groundwork for the later boom in AI computing power.

    概念：从提出到系统阐释

    2023 年 9 月在黑龙江首次提出“新质生产力”；同年中央经济工作会议提出以颠覆性技术和前沿技术催生新产业；2024 年 1 月，中央政治局集体学习给出了“三要素 + 优化组合 + 全要素生产率”的完整定义。

    The concept: from first mention to full definition

    “New quality productive forces” was first put forward in Heilongjiang in September 2023. Later that year, the Central Economic Work Conference called for disruptive and frontier technologies to give rise to new industries. In January 2024, a Politburo group study session set out the complete definition: three factors + optimal combination + total factor productivity.

    政策与基础设施：给 AI 铺路

    《算力基础设施高质量发展行动计划》提出到 2025 年算力规模超过 、智能算力占比达到 ；生成式 AI 管理办法、政府工作报告中的“人工智能+”，以及国发〔2025〕11号文件，构成了“AI 赋能千行百业”的政策骨架。

    Policy and infrastructure: paving the way for AI

    The Action Plan for the High-Quality Development of Computing Infrastructure set 2025 targets of more than in total computing power, with AI computing power making up of it. The interim measures on generative AI services, “AI Plus” in the Government Work Report and State Council document Guo Fa [2025] No. 11 together form the policy backbone for “AI empowering every industry”.

    就业：新职业被正式命名

    2024 年 7 月，人社部发布 新职业、新工种，其中就有“生成式人工智能系统应用员”——劳动者这一要素，正在被 AI 重新定义。

    Jobs: new occupations get official names

    In July 2024, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security announced new occupations and new job types, among them “generative AI system application specialist”. Workers, as a factor of production, are being redefined by AI.

    工具、应用与人的协作如何连接？

    How do tools, applications and workers connect?

    算力 → 模型

    智能算力（第 2 章）是训练大模型的燃料。没有算力，就没有“会思考的工具”。

    Compute → models

    AI computing power (Chapter 2) is the fuel for training large models. Without compute, there are no tools that “think”.

    模型 → 应用

    备案上线的模型（第 3 章）进入客服、编程、写作和千行百业，成为劳动者手中的新工具。

    Models → applications

    Models that have been filed and launched (Chapter 3) move into customer support, coding, writing and every other industry, becoming new tools in workers' hands.

    应用 → 数据 → 模型

    数以亿计的用户在使用中产生新的数据与反馈（第 4 章），又被用来改进模型。循环闭合，飞轮开始加速。

    Applications → data → models

    Hundreds of millions of users generate new data and feedback as they use these tools (Chapter 4), which in turn are used to improve the models. The loop closes and the flywheel starts to speed up.

    劳动者居于中心

    新职业、新技能（第 5 章）让人能驾驭这一循环。人机协同的劳动者，是把三要素“优化组合”起来的关键。

    Workers at the centre

    New occupations and new skills (Chapter 5) let people steer this loop. Workers who collaborate with AI are the key to bringing the three factors into an “optimal combination”.
